How it works

Three steps. Under a second.

1

Select text

Highlight text in any app. Email, Slack, your code editor, a browser, anywhere.

2

Press the hotkey

Cmd+Shift+E on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+E on Windows/Linux.

3

Fixed instantly

Grammar, spelling, and style corrections replace your selection in place.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

🌐

Works in every app

System-wide correction, not a browser extension. Works in your editor, email client, Slack, terminal, and everywhere else.

✈️

Offline-first AI

Built-in T5 grammar model runs entirely on your device. No API keys, no internet, no data leaving your machine.

🔄

Multi-provider

Use the local model or connect Claude, GPT, or Gemini through their CLI tools. Switch providers in one click.

🚦

Real-time monitoring

A floating traffic light indicator shows writing quality as you type. Green, yellow, or red with one-click fixes.

💻

Developer-friendly

Preserves code blocks, URLs, @mentions, :emoji:, email addresses, and file paths. Never mangles your tokens.

🌍

13 languages + tone presets

Auto-detect or choose from 13 languages. Five tone presets: default, casual, professional, academic, and Slack.
